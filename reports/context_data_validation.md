## Objective

The objective of this validation process is to verify the integrity and consistency of the merged contextual dataset generated by combining machine telemetry data with external contextual information. The validation ensures that the dataset is reliable and suitable for downstream feature engineering, predictive modeling, and machine learning workflows.
